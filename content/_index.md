Hello, Internet.

I’m an ML Engineer at [Bridgewater AIA Labs](https://www.bridgewater.com/), where I work on training LLMs for forecasting. My work has followed two threads: building and evaluating code agents, and creating open AI models from data that anyone can lawfully use.

The agents thread started at [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com), where I was a Staff ML Engineer. I was responsible for evaluating a Java migration agent that launched in preview with select Cloud partners in January 2025, and that evaluation work became [FreshBrew](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04852) ([ICSE 2026](https://conf.researchr.org/home/icse-2026)). I then moved to [Gemini CLI](https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli), where I implemented [parts of its core agent logic](https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Amrcabbage972+is%3Amerged) and the routing between Gemini Pro and Flash. The routing was well received by the community and was built on a routing evaluation framework I developed internally. I also collaborated with the Google DeepMind code pillar and the frontier evals team on SWE task environments and internal benchmarks for Gemini. Designing Gemini CLI meant watching the harness behave differently with each new Gemini version, and that experience led to [Agents Learn Their Runtime](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01209). Along the way I also co-authored [GitChameleon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12367) ([ACL 2026](https://2026.aclweb.org/)), a benchmark of code generation under Python library version constraints.

The second thread started in my free time. The initial release of ChatGPT prompted me to join the open-source community, working toward AI that is democratized, lawful and safe. That work produced [Aurora-M](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00399), an open multilingual language and code model, and [MixtureVitae](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25531), an open permissive-first pretraining corpus that matches non-permissive baselines at a fraction of the token budget ([TMLR](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/) with Featured and J2C Certifications, presented at ICML 2026). Its follow-up, *Strong Post-Training from Permissive, Reasoning-Dominant, Web-Scale Pretraining*, was accepted to NeurIPS 2026.

Earlier in my career, I led a team at [Chegg](https://www.chegg.com) fine-tuning vision-language models, and worked on recommender systems and multilingual NLP at [Taboola](https://www.taboola.com/).

I hold an M.Sc. in Applied Mathematics from [Tel Aviv University](https://english.tau.ac.il/) and a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Mathematics from [Bar-Ilan University](https://www.biu.ac.il/en).
